# UI asset sheet: src/assets/ui/ui-sheet-3x.png

A 3072x3072 sheet drawn at 3x (every art pixel is a solid 3x3 block), so 1024x1024 art pixels. Coordinates below are art pixels (`x, y, w x h`), the ones `scripts/slice-ui-sheet.js` uses. Background is transparent. The palette is shared across the sheet: dark plum outline `#3d2936`, rose rims `#d09c8e` / `#bd6a62` / `#8f4d57`, peach `#ffc2a1`, slate `#686f99` / `#404973` / `#a3a7c2`, gold `#f0b541` / `#cf752b`, teal gems `#92e8c0` / `#4fa4b8`, red `#e64539`, green `#63ab3f` / `#c8d45d`.

To look at a region, scale it down to 1x (sample every third pixel) and crop; the art is too small to read at the sheet's own size in a downscaled preview.

## Used in the game

`slice-ui-sheet.js` writes: `frame-parchment.png` (peach frame), `frame-banner.png` / `-red` / `-green` (the slate frame, recolored dark for light text), `name-plate.png` (rose gem plate, 13 rows), `hand-pointer.png` and `hand-pointer-down.png`, `bar-frame.png` (gauge frame), `arrow-down.png` / `arrow-up.png`, `button.png` / `button-hover.png` / `button-pressed.png` (the small rose button's three states, 18x13 at (151, 162), (183, 162), (215, 162), each cut with a 1px margin for the glow), `checkbox.png` / `checkbox-checked.png` (rose checkbox, empty and with its teal gem), `slider-knob.png` (the orange 8x8 bead at (436, 228)), `item-slot.png` (the empty rose item slot at (0, 112)), and 15 glyphs in `src/assets/glyphs/` (doubled to 16x16; the script lists which icon each kind uses). The bar fill strips' colors (light row over dark: green `#c8d45d`/`#63ab3f`, teal `#92e8c0`/`#4fa4b8`) are the gauges' `--hp-fill` / `--mp-fill` in ui.css. Unused so far: the wide, gold and "OK" buttons, the tab icons and the slot tiles with icons, the dividers (the plain rose one is `--rule`'s color already), the button prompts, the fonts, the books and the pennants.
